## Tolerance budget

Each row is the LARGEST swept value the loop still tolerates -- it locks and the fractional spur stays at or below -60 dBc. The next step up was confirmed to fail on every seed. These are what set device areas -- convert to geometry with the PDK's mismatch parameters, then confirm by Monte-Carlo in ngspice.

`>=` means the sweep never found a failure, so the entry is a lower bound and that imperfection is not the binding constraint.

Each point is run on several seeds. For the mismatch sweeps the seed is the device draw, so the spur reported is the WORST of them -- these are device areas, and sizing from the lucky draw is how a budget silently becomes optimistic. With three seeds that is a crude upper quartile, not a yield number; a real budget wants Monte-Carlo at a stated confidence.

|---|---|---|
| `dac_sigma_lsb` | 0.025 LSB rms | unit current-source area (Pelgrom) |
| `dac_gain_err` | 0.20 % | ramp mirror vs DAC reference tracking; calibration range |
